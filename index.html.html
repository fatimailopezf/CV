About me

I work in the space between the people who build technology and the organisations that can put it to use. Trained in medical physics (PhD, University of Sheffield), I've built that bridge in hospitals, health-policy consulting, a business school, Mexico's national public health system and now a leading AI research centre.

At the Computer Vision Centre in Barcelona, I lead partnerships and take AI projects with industry from first idea to pilot. I mentor AI researchers on turning technology into ventures, and I advise, pro bono, a Mexico–Barcelona initiative on AI in health. I work in English, Spanish and Catalan, across Europe and the Americas.

What I'm looking for: a director role in innovation, business development or strategic alliances, in a research centre, university or company with an international reach, remote or hybrid.

AI projects

At the CVC I've worked on AI projects across cultural heritage, mobility, audiovisual and creativity, agrifood and industry 4.0. Computer vision cuts across industries, which is why my work spans so many sectors. Here are three examples.

Computer vision that detects algae and hydrocarbons in water plants

JointLab between the CVC and an industry partner, since 2022

The problem
Water plants need to know when algae or hydrocarbons are present. The solution uses computer vision.
What I did
Coordinated with researchers and engineers in computer vision to prepare the technical and economic offer, and managed budget, resources and deliverables.
Where it stands
An MVP piloted in a couple of plants, now being improved to reach more. Funded with €125k in year one, then extended for three more years to tackle new challenges, for a total of €250k since 2022.

An alarm system for waste overflowing around Barcelona's containers

With an innovation cooperative for waste collection and street cleaning, since 2024

The problem
Oversee urban waste around containers across Barcelona, and raise an alarm when it overflows.
What I did
Main contact with the cooperative, led the public-funding proposal, and coordinated budgets, work packages and deliverables with the engineering teams.
Where it stands
A first functional prototype is piloting in the city, funded with €250k. At scale, it should reduce waste around Barcelona and improve how its streets look.

A system to detect fake news

Current project

The problem
Detecting fake news, a need for news media, broadcasting, reputation consultancy and branding.
What I do
I manage IP protection and pitch preparation, look for industry partners to run a pilot, seek public funding to raise the technology readiness level, and involve the technical team in accelerator programmes, including AI accelerators.
Where it stands
Working with two local media and broadcast companies to run a pilot, while looking for public funding to increase technology readiness.
